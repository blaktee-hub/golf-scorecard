Privacy Policy

Golf Scorecard

Golf Scorecard does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

The app saves the following on your device only, using your browser or device's local storage:

  • The round you are currently playing — player names, scores, and game settings
  • Courses you add yourself, including their name and hole pars
  • Rounds you have finished, kept as your saved history

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server, not backed up to us, and not visible to the developer or to anyone else. No account is required and none is offered.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind
  • No third-party services, software development kits, or embedded trackers
  • No location or GPS access
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data
  • No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected

Sharing a scorecard

When you tap Share, the app hands a plain-text summary of that round to your device's own share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app does not send it anywhere on its own and keeps no copy of what you shared.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Deleting your data

You control everything the app stores. Delete a saved round with the × on the Rounds screen, remove a course you added with the − button, and clear the current card with New Round. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device. There is nothing stored elsewhere for us to delete.

Changes
